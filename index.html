Integralni model razvoja jezikov

Integralna teorija razvoja jezikov
Integral Theory of Language Development

Ali lahko zgodovino evropskih in drugih starih jezikov razumemo tudi drugače kot skozi model enotnega praindoevropskega prajezika?

Integralna teorija raziskuje možnost, da je jezikovni razvoj potekal skozi dolgotrajne procese prepletanja narečij, jezikovnih stikov, prevzemanja besedišča, pomenskih prenosov ter vplivanja starejših jezikovnih plasti.

Kratka zgodovina sodobnega jezikoslovja

Do konca 18. stoletja, torej dokler se niso seznanili s sanskrtom, so evropski učenjaki verjeli, da vsi jeziki na svetu izvirajo iz hebrejščine, od nje pa so se oddaljili in razpršili po zrušitvi babilonskega stolpa. Svet sam naj bi bil star komaj 6000 let. Po izračunu Jamesa Ussherja, nadškofa iz Armagha (1654) bi naj nastal natančno 22. oktobra 4004 pr. n. št.. Kdor je dvomil, je tvegal obsodbo. Tudi razumevanje slovnice v Evropi je bilo omejeno na grško in latinsko tradicijo. Zanašali so se predvsem na Aristotela, ki je v 4. st. pr. n. št. jasno ločil pojme onoma (samostalnik) in rhema (glagol) ter opisal sklone (ptosis – padež), na Dionizija Tračana, ki je v 2. st. pr. n. št. zapisal osem besednih vrst, ter na Donata in Prisciana, ki sta grška izhodišča prenesla na latinščino. Ko so Evropejci spoznali sanskrtsko slovnico, so doživeli pravi kulturni šok. Še posebej jih je navdušilo delo Aṣṭādhyāyī (अष्टाध्यायी), kjer je indijski jezikoslovec Pāṇini sredi 1. tisočletja pr. n. št. sanskrt z vsemi podrobnostmi opisal tako popolno, da še danes velja za nedosegljivo mojstrovino. Sanskrt jim ni prinesel le novih besed, ampak povsem nov način razmišljanja o jezikih:

Koncept glagolskega korena (dhātu, धातु): To je bila za evropske jezikoslovce revolucija. Spoznali so, da se iz približno 2000 korenov s priponami tvori celotno bogastvo jezika. Ta zamisel je postala temelj primerjalne indoevropske lingvistike.

Natančno fonetiko: Indijski gramatiki so imeli izjemno natančno razčlenitev abecednih glasov, ki je daleč presegla grško-latinsko tradicijo. Njihov red glasov v abecedi (Varnamālā वर्णमाला) ni bil poljuben, ampak je temeljil na anatomskih in akustičnih lastnostih glasov, po mestu tvorbe: velarni, palatalni, retrofleksni, dentalni, labialni.

Morfologijo in sklone (kāraka कारक): Medtem ko so Evropejci poznali zgolj štiri ali pet sklonov (imenovalnik, rodilnik...), je Pāṇinijev sistem kāraka s sedmimi skloni ponujal veliko bolj natančen vpogled v to, kako glagol upravlja pomene v stavku.

Natančne glasovne zakone (sandhi सन्धि; redukcija, vokalizacija likvid, nazalne izmene).

Generativni pristop: Pāṇinijeva slovnica je bila že pred več kot dvema tisočletjema izjemno natančna. Zbirka pravil (sūtra सूत्र) namreč določa vse pravilne oblike jezika iz osnovnih elementov, brez izjem.

Zahod je bil postavljen pred dejstvo, da je vedski jezik veliko bolj kompleksen, kot latinščina in grščina.

Kaj ima sanskrt, česar nimata grščina in latinščina

Sanskrt ima osem sklonov: imenovalnik, rodilnik, dajalnik, tožilnik, mestnik, orodnik, zvalnik in ločilnik (ablativ). Grščina nima orodnika, mestnika in ločilnika (njihove vloge sta prevzela rodilnik in dajalnik). Latinščina je združila ločilnik, mestnik in orodnik v en sam sklon – latinski ablativ (lokativ se je ohranil le v redkih ostankih, npr. domi – doma).

Sanskrt dosledno uporablja dvojino pri vseh samostalnikih, zaimkih, pridevnikih in glagolih, kadar gre za par. Stara grščina dvojino sicer pozna, vendar jo v klasični dobi uporablja redko in postopoma opušča, latinščina pa jo je (razen v redkih besedah, kot sta duo in ambo) povsem izgubila.

Sanskrt ima pet mest tvorbe soglasnikov (grleni, nebniki, retrofleksni, zobni, ustnični) in štiri različice za vsako mesto (nezveneči, nezveneči pridihnjeni, zveneči, zveneči pridihnjeni – npr. k, kh, g, gh). Grščina je izgubila zveneče pridihnjene soglasnike (spremenili so se v nezveneče pridihnjene ph, th, kh), latinščina pa je pridihnjene soglasnike v celoti izgubila ali spremenila v pripornike (f, h).

V sanskrtu se končni glas besede in začetni glas naslednje besede obvezno zlijeta in spremenita tako v govorjenem jeziku kot v pisavi (npr. tat + ca postane tacca). Grščina in latinščina poznata le posamezne asimilacije znotraj besed ali redke elizije, ne pa celovitega sistema zunanje glasovne prilagoditve.

Poleg povednika, velelnika, konjunktiva in optativa ima najstarejši sanskrt še peti glagolski naklon – injunktiv (za izražanje brezčasnih dejstev ali prepovedi). Grščina ima štiri naklone (brez injunktiva), latinščina pa le tri (povednik, velelnik in konjunktiv, ki je združil stari konjunktiv in optativ).

Zahodni jezikoslovci so bili s sanskrtom postavljeni pred veliko dilemo. Vse je namreč kazalo, da je sanskrt veliko starejši kot grščina in latinščina in da je tudi vedsko jezikoslovje veliko bolj razvito kot zahodno. Hkrati je vedska književnost izredno bogata in obsežna, Mahabharata, recimo, je desetkrat daljša kot Homerjevi Ilijada in Odiseja skupaj. Tudi bogatstvo sopomenk in metafor v sanskrtu je nekajkrat bolj obsežno kot v grščini in latinščini skupaj. Vedska astronomija je bila še bolj natančna od grške, recimo, glede izmere obsega zemeljske krogle.

Kaj ima vedska astronomija, česar grška in rimska nista imeli?

Siderski zodiak in merjenje precesije (ajanamša): Zaradi nagiba Zemljine osi se točka pomladnega enakonočja počasi premika glede na zvezde v ozadju. Grki in Rimljani so fiksirali zodiak na letne čase (tropski zodiak), zaradi česar se nebesna znamenja danes ne ujemajo več z dejanskimi ozvezdji. Vedska astronomija uporablja siderski sistem – izračune nenehno popravlja z vrednostjo ajanamše, zato se položaji planetov vedno ujemajo z resničnimi zvezdami.

Sistem 27 luninih hiš (nakšater): Medtem ko sta grška in rimska tradicija nebo razdelili na 12 sončevih znamenj, ga je vedska razdelila na 27 ozvezdij (vsako meri 13°20’), skozi katera potuje Luna vsak dan. To omogoča veliko natančnejše sledenje luninim ciklom in koledarskim izračunom.

Desetiški sistem, ničla in napredna trigonometrija: Vedske astronomske razprave (Siddhante, npr. Surja Siddhanta) so za izračun planetnih tirov uporabljale mestni desetiški sistem z ničlo ter razvile sodobne trigonometrične funkcije (sinus – džja, kosinus – kotidžja). Grki in Rimljani so računali z nerodnimi črkovnimi ali rimskimi številkami ter v geometriji uporabljali tetive kroga namesto sinusa.

Globoki kozmični časovni cikli (juge): Grško-rimska antika je svet običajno razumela kot nekaj tisoč let star in prostorsko omejen na geocentrične kristalne sfere. Vedska kozmologija pozna nepredstavljivo dolge časovne cikle – en dan Brahme (kalpa) traja 4,32 milijarde let, kar presenetljivo sovpada s sodobno oceno starosti Zemlje.

Presežki vedske filozofije v primerjavi z evropsko

Evropska filozofija (od predsokratikov in Platona do moderne dobe) se primarno sprašuje, kaj lahko razum spozna o zunanjem svetu, in pogosto temelji na ostri ločitvi med opazovalcem in opazovanim. Vedska filozofija (Vedanta, Sankhja, Joga) pa izhaja iz raziskovanja same zavesti.

Zavest kot temelj resničnosti (ne stranski produkt): Zahodna filozofija in znanost se še danes spopadata s »težkim problemom zavesti« – kako iz mrtve snovi nastane subjektivno doživljanje. Vedska tradicija obrne izhodišče: čista zavest (Brahman ali Puruša) je prvotna in edina absolutna resničnost, snovni svet (Prakriti) pa je njena pojavna oblika.

Neposredna izkušnja namesto zgolj intelekta: V Evropi je filozofija pretežno akademska, diskurzivna disciplina – iskanje resnice z logiko in jezikom. V vedskem izročilu logika (Njaja) služi le kot priprava; vrhunec filozofije je neposredno izkustveno spoznanje (aparokša anubhuti), ki ga posameznik doseže s sistematičnimi tehnikami meditacije in umiritve uma (Joga).

Natančna anatomija uma (antahkarana): Zahodna psihologija in filozofija uma sta dolgo obravnavali um kot enotno celoto ali pa šele v 20. stoletju odkrili podzavest. Vedska misel že tisočletja natančno loči štiri funkcije notranjega instrumenta:

  • Manas (čutni, odzivni um, ki zbira vtise),
  • Buddhi (razločevalni intelekt in modrost),
  • Ahamkara (ego – načelo, ki izkušnje pripiše »meni«),
  • Čitta (skladišče podzavestnih vtisov in spominov – samskar).

Odprava dualizma med subjektom in objektom: Descartesova delitev na mislečo substanco (res cogitans) in razsežno snov (res extensa) je zaznamovala evropsko misel z globokim razcepom med človekom in naravo. Vedska nedualistična filozofija (Advaita Vedanta) to ločitev razgradi kot iluzijo (maja) in trdi, da sta najgloblje jedro posameznika (Atman) in izvor vesolja (Brahman) v resnici eno in isto.

Zakon vzročnosti brez zunanjega sodnika (Karma): Namesto zahodnega pojmovanja nagrajevanja in kaznovanja s strani zunanjega božanstva ali slepe usode (fatum), vedska filozofija uvaja karmo kot popolnoma neoseben, naraven zakon vzroka in posledice na ravni misli, namere in dejanja.

Očitna inferiornost evropske kulture v primerjavi z indijsko je bila hud udarec za zahodno Evropo, še posebej za tiste narode, ki so imeli vcepljen občutek večvrednosti, predvsem zaradi učinkovitih vojaških tehnologij, ki so jim omogočil izkoriščanje drugih narodov preko kolonializma in suženjstva.

Morda najhuje jim je bilo prenašati vedske podatke o starosti samih ved, ki bi naj bile starejše od samega nastanka sveta po bibliji.

Seveda pa pisna beseda sama po sebi še ni dokaz. Marsikaj je že bilo izrečeno in napisano, pa sploh ni bilo resnično. To so na zahodu dobro vedeli, saj je ena najstarejših disciplin grške filozofije, sofizem, preučevala prav to, namreč, kako z besedami dokazati nekaj, kar samo po sebi ni dokazljivo. Sofizem je v osnovi umetnost in tehnika prepričevanja, argumentacije in razpravljanja z jezikom.

Evropski sofizem

Sofisti (npr. Protagora, Gorgija, Trazimah) so izhajali iz dvoma, da ima človek neposreden, objektiven dostop do absolutne resnice ali nespremenljivih naravnih dejstev v razpravi.

V okviru klasičnega sofističnega ideala lahko izurjen sofist zagovarja katerokoli stališče, tudi takšno, ki je materialno nedokazljivo ali splošno nesprejeto. Ena temeljnih vaj sofističnih šol je bila prav antilogija (sposobnost sestaviti enako prepričljiva govora za dve popolnoma nasprotni trditvi).

Protagorov načelo človeka kot mere vseh stvari: Če je resnica za vsakega posameznika ali skupnost odvisna od zaznave, položaja in okoliščin, potem ni enega samega »dejstva«, ki bi enkrat za vselej preglasovalo vsak drug pogled. Cilj govorca je, da šibkejši argument naredi močnejšega – torej da prepriča poslušalce, sodnike ali skupščino.

Gorgijeva moč besede (logos): Gorgija je jezik primerjal z mogočno silo, ki deluje na dušo podobno kot zdravilo ali strup na telo. Beseda lahko očara, prepriča in preoblikuje mnenje, ne glede na to, ali preprosto poroča o zunanjem dejstvu.

Da bi trditev brez materialne podlage postala prepričljiva, sofist uporabi štiri glavne jezikovne mehanizme:

  1. Preokvirjanje pomena besed: Isto dejanje poimenuje z drugo besedo – nespametno drznost imenuje pogum, strahopetno oklevanje preudarnost, trmo pa načelnost. Ko poslušalec sprejme besedo, sprejme tudi sodbo.
  2. Izkoriščanje dvoumnosti in skritih predpostavk: Zastavi vprašanje ali trditev, ki že vnaprej vsebuje nepreverjeno predpostavko, tako da se razprava preseli na podrobnosti, namesto da bi preverjala temeljno dejstvo.
  3. Analogni sklepi: Neznano ali nedokazljivo stvar primerja z nečim znanim in očitnim iz vsakdanjega življenja, s čimer ustvari občutek, da velja enaka zakonitost.
  4. Ritem, slog in čustveni naboj: Kot je trdil Gorgija, ritem stavkov, nasprotja (antiteze) in metafore vplivajo na poslušalčevo čustveno presojo, še preden razum preveri, ali trditev sploh stoji.

Čeprav lahko dober sofist zagovarja vsako stališče, ne more vedno prepričati vsakega občinstva. Njegova uspešnost je omejena z dvema dejavnikoma:

Okvir občinstva (doxa): Besede delujejo le, če se naslonijo na obstoječa prepričanja, vrednote ali strahove poslušalcev. Če trditev preveč neposredno trči ob neposredno čutno izkušnjo občinstva, besedna spretnost pogosto odpove.

Nasprotnik enake moči: V javni razpravi ali na sodišču proti sofistu pogosto stoji drug sofist, ki uporabi enako tehniko v nasprotno smer. Takrat odloči, katera zgodba je bolj skladna in psihološko sprejemljiva.

V praksi torej sofizem na področju nedokazljivega deluje kot arhitektura prepričanja: kjer ni materialnih temeljev, zgradi konstrukcijo iz verjetnosti, jezika in psihologije poslušalca, ki stoji dovolj trdno, da jo ljudje sprejmejo kot resnično.

Ker je nemogoče, da bi se kdo vrnil v preteklost in preveril, kaj se je dogajalo pred tisočletji, je odnos med sanskrtom ter zahodnimi jeziki izredno hvaležen primer za uporabo sofističnih načel.

Zahodni jezikoslovci so gradili na obstoječih prepričanjih, vrednotah in strahovih, ki so zahodnim Evropejcem narekovale, da so drugi narodi manj vredni kot oni sami, to nalogo pa so seveda najlažje opravili ravno univerzitetni profesorji z veliko avtoriteto.

PIE teorija

Tako so evropski jezikoslovci 19. stoletja preuokvirili sanskrt iz pradedka mnogih jezikov v enega izmed bratrancev. Na mesto pradedka pa so narisali nek do tedaj neznan jezik do tedaj neznanega naroda, ki so ga imenoval Praindoevropejci ali Protoindoevropejci (PIE). Intenzivno so preučili sanskrtska pravila in jih preinterpretirali v smislu časovnega razvoja iz domnevnega praindoevropskega jezika. Glasovne razlike med grščino in latinščino ter sanskrtom so, kjer je bilo le mogoče, reinterpretirali tako, da so grškim in latinskim glasovom pripisali višjo starost, sanskrtske glasove pa označili kot kasnejše inovacije.

Da bi vse skupaj bilo prepričljivo, so morali sanskrtu samemu pripisati seveda veliko mlajši izvor. Mejnik starosti, da bi bila njihova razlaga še teoretično izvedljiva, je postala letnica 1500 – 1300 pr n št za nastanek Rg vede. V Rgvedi sami se sicer nahajajo astronomski podatki, ki jasno kažejo na starost vsaj 6000 let, kar z enim zamahom izniči celotno konstrukcijo zahodnega jezikoslovja.

Kakšna pa je ta zahodna konstrukcija razvoja jezikov, ki jo pogosto označujejo kar za “uradno” ali vsaj z opisom “sprejeta s strani večine strokovnjakov” ali “najbolj verjetna po mnenju velike večine strokovnjakov”?

Na kratko pravi, da so približno 4000 ali 3500 pr n št stepska ljudstva s konji prišla v Evropo in prinesla s seboj praindoevropski (PIE) jezik, ki bi ga naj kar vsi sprejeli, saj so se bali teh stepskih jezdecev, oziroma bi jih naj želeli posnemati. Isto bi se naj zgodilo v Indiji in zato bi si naj bili jeziki podobni. V tem smislu, bi naj imeli Protoindoevropejci v Indiji podobno vlogo, kot so si jo v Indiji pripisovali Britanci, ki so se videli kot prinašalci razsvetljenja in razvoja, ne da bi pomislil, da je bila britanska kolonizacija Indije za Indijo katastrofalna, saj je iz svetovne velesile padla na stopnjo tretjega sveta, Britanija pa je obratno, iz dežele tretjega sveta zrasla v svetovno velesilo.

Od kod potem razlike med recimo germanskimi, slovanskimi in romanskimi jeziki po teoriji PIE? Po njihovem bi naj Germani in Romani (in Kelti ter Grki) s svojimi jeziki prvi zapustili pradomovino v stepah in nadvladali Evropo. Potem bi naj začelo prihajati do radikalnih jezikovnih sprememb, ampak ne toliko v Evropi, kjer bi se naj srečali različni jeziki, tam bi naj domačini kar brez nadaljnega sprejeli nov jezik v svoji izvorni obliki. V stepski “pradomovini” pa bi naj medtem prišlo do dramatičnih glasovnih sprememb, kar tako, brez kakršnih koli stikov s komer koli, kar samo od sebe. In ravno medtem, ko bi se naj te spremembe dogajale, bi se naj tisti, ki so ostali v stepski “pradomovini”, še dodatno razcepili. Eni bi naj šli v Indijo in Perzijo, kjer bi naj spet vsi milijoni tamkajšnjih prebivalcev popolnoma brez odpora sprejeli njihov stepski jezik, in ga v kratkem razvili v najbolj popoln jezik na svetu in spisali najbolj obsežno in kompleksno mitologijo na svetu. Drugi bi naj šli do Baltika in tam razvili trgovino z jantarjem, tretji pa bi se naj za dolga tisočletja skrili v pripjatska močvirja in prilezli na plano šele v srednjem veku, ravno ko je bila po vsej Evropi vojna med Rimom in perzijskimi zavezniki (Goti, Sarmati, Avari itd). V hipu, ko bi se naj ti močvirniki začeli razširjati po Evropi, bi naj njihov jezik začel razpadati na jezike in narečja, dokler ne bi recimo v Sloveniji, obstajalo več kot 40 narečij, kot jih imamo danes.

Seveda je to ob dejstvu, da preteklost ni zlahka dokazljiva, popolnoma legitimna teorija. Vprašanje je le, če je prepričljiva.

Ob predpostavki, da pa je Rg veda pravzaprav verjetno pa le stara 6000 let, takšna razlaga seveda takoj odpade kot nesmiselna, saj so stepski jezdeci prišli v Indijo šele nekaj malega predi 1300-1500 pr n š.

Kako pa se je pravzaprav prišlo do datuma 1500 pr n št za Rg vedo? No, treba je povedati, da je to ideja Maxa Mullerja, ki je sam priznal, da je to zgolj njegovo mnenje in pravzaprav ugibanje.

Pogosto se kot dokaz za pravilnost PIE teorije jemlje profesorja sanskrta De Saussureja, ki je menil, da bi moral jezik, iz katerega bi naj nastali indoevropski jeziki, imeti pred določenimi samoglasniki neke grlene glasove, podobne glasu H. Do tega prepričanja je prišel, ko je iskal razloge, zakaj imajo določene besede v sanskrtu glas A oziroma polglasnik, grščina in latinščina pa imata na istih mestih (v sorodnih besedah) samoglasnik O, E ali pa A. Ker je bil prepričan, da je sanskrt mlajši, je torej predvideval, da je prednik vseh teh jezikov imel tri vrste takšnih grlenih glasov, ki so v vseh jezikih potomcih izginili, so pa vplivali na izgovorjavo samoglasnikov, ki so ostali. Tako je presodil, da je sanskrt izgubil tisti grleni glas in dodatno poenotil vse samoglasnike v A, medtem ko bi naj latinščina in grščina sicer prav tako izgubili tisti grleni glas, hkrati pa bi naj ohranili izvorni samoglasnik, in zato bi naj besede v latinščini in grščini “ohranile” prvotne A-je, E-je ali O-je ne pa tudi v sanskrtu. Koliko logično je sklepati, da je sanskrt na neki točki degradiral, latinščina in grščina pa bi naj ohranili stare oblike, je seveda vprašanje zase, še posebno, če je sanskrt očitno ohranil cel kup starodavnih arhaizmov, ki jih grščina in latinščina nista, a pustimo to ta hip. Kar je pomembno, je dejstvo, da so nekaj let zatem dešifrirali hetitski jezik in tam dejansko našli neke grlene glasove pred samoglasniki. Nikogar pa ni zanimalo, da so v hetitščini našli zgolj en sam tak grleni glas, medtem, ko je De Saussure predvideval tri podobne glasove. En glas namreč nima vpliva na razliko med O-ji, E-ji in A-ji in je za De Saussurjevo tezo neuporaben. Ampak to očitno ni tako pomembno, saj se zadnjih sto let vztrajno ponavlja "parola", da je De Saussure napovedal hetitske laringale, ki bi naj “dokazali” laringalno teorijo. Ta pravi, da so anatolski jeziki najstarejši indoevropski jeziki, takoj za njimi po starosti bi naj bili zahodni evropski jeziki, najmlajši pa bi naj po tej logiki seveda bili sanskrt, iranski, baltski in slovanski jeziki. Od teh bi naj bili nastarejši baltski, potem indoiranski in seveda popolnpma na koncu, slovenski jeziki.

Kako pa gleda na jezikovni razvoj integralna teorija?

Integralna teorija je pravzaprav zelo enostavna. Integral Model of Language Development

Integral Theory of Language Development

Can the history of European and other ancient languages be understood differently than through the model of a single Proto-Indo-European proto-language?

The Integral Theory explores the possibility that language development occurred through long-term processes of dialect intertwining, language contact, vocabulary borrowing, semantic transfers, and the influence of older linguistic layers.

A Brief History of Modern Linguistics

Until the end of the 18th century, that is, until they became acquainted with Sanskrit, European scholars believed that all languages in the world originated from Hebrew, from which they had diverged and scattered after the collapse of the Tower of Babel. The world itself was believed to be barely 6000 years old. According to the calculation of James Ussher, Archbishop of Armagh (1654), it was created precisely on October 22, 4004 BC. Anyone who doubted this risked condemnation. The understanding of grammar in Europe was also limited to the Greek and Latin tradition. They relied primarily on Aristotle, who in the 4th century BC clearly distinguished the concepts of onoma (noun) and rhema (verb) and described cases (ptosis – case), on Dionysius Thrax, who in the 2nd century BC recorded eight parts of speech, and on Donatus and Priscian, who transferred Greek starting points to Latin. When Europeans became acquainted with Sanskrit grammar, they experienced a true cultural shock. They were particularly impressed by the work Aṣṭādhyāyī (अष्टाध्यायी), in which the Indian linguist Pāṇini in the mid-1st millennium BC described Sanskrit with all its details so perfectly that it is still considered an unattainable masterpiece today. Sanskrit brought them not only new words but an entirely new way of thinking about languages:

The concept of the verbal root (dhātu, धातु): This was a revolution for European linguists. They realized that the entire wealth of a language is formed from approximately 2000 roots with suffixes. This idea became the foundation of comparative Indo-European linguistics.

Precise phonetics: Indian grammarians had an extremely precise articulation of alphabetical sounds, which far exceeded the Greek-Latin tradition. Their order of sounds in the alphabet (Varnamālā वर्णमाला) was not arbitrary but was based on the anatomical and acoustic properties of sounds, by place of articulation: velar, palatal, retroflex, dental, labial.

Morphology and cases (kāraka कारक): While Europeans knew only four or five cases (nominative, genitive...), Pāṇini's kāraka system with seven cases offered a much more precise insight into how a verb governs meanings in a sentence.

Precise phonetic laws (sandhi सन्धि; reduction, vocalization of liquids, nasal changes).

Generative approach: Pāṇini's grammar was extremely precise more than two millennia ago. The collection of rules (sūtra सूत्र) determines all correct forms of the language from basic elements, without exceptions.

The West was confronted with the fact that the Vedic language is far more complex than Latin and Greek.

What Sanskrit Has That Greek and Latin Do Not

Sanskrit has eight cases: nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, locative, instrumental, vocative, and ablative. Greek lacks the instrumental, locative, and ablative (their roles were taken over by the genitive and dative). Latin merged the ablative, locative, and instrumental into a single case – the Latin ablative (the locative survived only in rare remnants, e.g., domi – at home).

Sanskrit consistently uses the dual for all nouns, pronouns, adjectives, and verbs when referring to a pair. Ancient Greek knows the dual but uses it rarely in the classical period and gradually abandons it, while Latin has completely lost it (except in rare words such as duo and ambo).

Sanskrit has five places of consonant articulation (velar, palatal, retroflex, dental, labial) and four variants for each place (voiceless, voiceless aspirated, voiced, voiced aspirated – e.g., k, kh, g, gh). Greek lost the voiced aspirated consonants (they changed into voiceless aspirated ph, th, kh), while Latin completely lost the aspirated consonants or changed them into fricatives (f, h).

In Sanskrit, the final sound of a word and the initial sound of the following word obligatorily merge and change both in spoken language and in writing (e.g., tat + ca becomes tacca). Greek and Latin know only individual assimilations within words or rare elisions, but not a complete system of external phonetic adaptation.

In addition to the indicative, imperative, conjunctive, and optative, the oldest Sanskrit has a fifth verbal mood – the injunctive (for expressing timeless facts or prohibitions). Greek has four moods (without the injunctive), and Latin only three (indicative, imperative, and conjunctive, which merged the old conjunctive and optative).

Western linguists were confronted with a great dilemma by Sanskrit. Everything indicated that Sanskrit was much older than Greek and Latin and that Vedic linguistics was also much more developed than Western. At the same time, Vedic literature is extremely rich and extensive; the Mahabharata, for example, is ten times longer than Homer's Iliad and Odyssey combined. The wealth of synonyms and metaphors in Sanskrit is also several times more extensive than in Greek and Latin combined. Vedic astronomy was even more precise than Greek, for example, regarding the measurement of the Earth's circumference.

What Vedic Astronomy Had That Greek and Roman Did Not

Sidereal zodiac and measurement of precession (ayanamsha): Due to the tilt of the Earth's axis, the point of the spring equinox slowly moves relative to the stars in the background. The Greeks and Romans fixed the zodiac to the seasons (tropical zodiac), which is why the celestial signs no longer match the actual constellations today. Vedic astronomy uses the sidereal system – it constantly corrects calculations with the value of ayanamsha, so the positions of the planets always match the real stars.

The system of 27 lunar houses (nakshatras): While the Greek and Roman traditions divided the sky into 12 solar signs, the Vedic tradition divided it into 27 constellations (each measuring 13°20'), through which the Moon travels each day. This allows for much more precise tracking of lunar cycles and calendar calculations.

Decimal system, zero, and advanced trigonometry: Vedic astronomical treatises (Siddhantas, e.g., Surya Siddhanta) used the place-value decimal system with zero to calculate planetary orbits and developed modern trigonometric functions (sine – jya, cosine – kotijya). The Greeks and Romans calculated with cumbersome letter or Roman numerals and used chords of a circle instead of sine in geometry.

Deep cosmic time cycles (yugas): Greco-Roman antiquity usually understood the world as a few thousand years old and spatially limited to geocentric crystalline spheres. Vedic cosmology knows unimaginably long time cycles – one day of Brahma (kalpa) lasts 4.32 billion years, which surprisingly coincides with the modern estimate of the age of the Earth.

Excesses of Vedic Philosophy Compared to European

European philosophy (from the pre-Socratics and Plato to the modern era) primarily asks what the mind can know about the external world and often rests on a sharp separation between observer and observed. Vedic philosophy (Vedanta, Sankhya, Yoga), however, proceeds from the investigation of consciousness itself.

Consciousness as the foundation of reality (not a by-product): Western philosophy and science still grapple today with the "hard problem of consciousness" – how subjective experience arises from dead matter. The Vedic tradition reverses the starting point: pure consciousness (Brahman or Purusha) is the primary and only absolute reality, and the material world (Prakriti) is its phenomenal form.

Direct experience instead of mere intellect: In Europe, philosophy is predominantly an academic, discursive discipline – the search for truth through logic and language. In the Vedic tradition, logic (Nyaya) serves only as preparation; the pinnacle of philosophy is direct experiential knowledge (aparoksha anubhuti), which an individual achieves through systematic techniques of meditation and calming the mind (Yoga).

Precise anatomy of the mind (antahkarana): Western psychology and philosophy of mind long treated the mind as a unified whole or only discovered the subconscious in the 20th century. Vedic thought has for millennia precisely distinguished four functions of the inner instrument:

  • Manas (sensory, responsive mind that collects impressions),
  • Buddhi (discriminating intellect and wisdom),
  • Ahamkara (ego – the principle that attributes experiences to "me"),
  • Chitta (storehouse of subconscious impressions and memories – samskaras).

Abolition of the dualism between subject and object: Descartes' division into thinking substance (res cogitans) and extended matter (res extensa) marked European thought with a deep split between man and nature. Vedic non-dualistic philosophy (Advaita Vedanta) deconstructs this separation as illusion (maya) and claims that the deepest core of the individual (Atman) and the source of the universe (Brahman) are in reality one and the same.

The law of causality without an external judge (Karma): Instead of the Western conception of reward and punishment by an external deity or blind fate (fatum), Vedic philosophy introduces karma as a completely impersonal, natural law of cause and effect at the level of thought, intention, and action.

The obvious inferiority of European culture compared to Indian was a severe blow to Western Europe, especially for those nations that had an implanted sense of superiority, primarily due to effective military technologies that enabled them to exploit other nations through colonialism and slavery.

Perhaps the hardest thing for them to bear was the Vedic data on the age of the Vedas themselves, which were said to be older than the very creation of the world according to the Bible.

Of course, the written word alone is not yet proof. Much has already been said and written that was not true at all. The West knew this well, as one of the oldest disciplines of Greek philosophy, sophism, studied precisely this: how to prove with words something that is not provable in itself. Sophism is essentially the art and technique of persuasion, argumentation, and discussion with language.

European Sophism

The Sophists (e.g., Protagoras, Gorgias, Thrasymachus) proceeded from the doubt that man has direct, objective access to absolute truth or unchanging natural facts in discussion.

Within the framework of the classical sophistic ideal, a trained sophist can defend any position, even one that is materially unprovable or generally unaccepted. One of the fundamental exercises of sophistic schools was precisely antilogy (the ability to compose equally convincing speeches for two completely opposite claims).

Protagoras' principle of man as the measure of all things: If truth for each individual or community depends on perception, position, and circumstances, then there is no single "fact" that would once and for all override every other view. The goal of the speaker is to make the weaker argument stronger – that is, to persuade listeners, judges, or the assembly.

Gorgias' power of the word (logos): Gorgias compared language to a mighty force that acts on the soul similarly to medicine or poison on the body. The word can enchant, persuade, and transform opinion, regardless of whether it simply reports on an external fact.

To make a claim without material basis convincing, a sophist uses four main linguistic mechanisms:

  1. Reframing the meaning of words: He names the same action with a different word – foolish boldness he calls courage, cowardly hesitation prudence, and stubbornness principle. When the listener accepts the word, he also accepts the judgment.
  2. Exploiting ambiguity and hidden assumptions: He poses a question or claim that already contains an unverified assumption in advance, so that the discussion moves to details instead of verifying the fundamental fact.
  3. Analogous conclusions: He compares an unknown or unprovable thing with something known and obvious from everyday life, thereby creating the feeling that the same law applies.
  4. Rhythm, style, and emotional charge: As Gorgias argued, the rhythm of sentences, contrasts (antitheses), and metaphors influence the listener's emotional judgment even before reason checks whether the claim stands at all.

Although a good sophist can defend any position, he cannot always convince every audience. His success is limited by two factors:

The framework of the audience (doxa): Words work only if they lean on the existing beliefs, values, or fears of the listeners. If a claim too directly collides with the audience's immediate sensory experience, verbal skill often fails.

An opponent of equal strength: In public debate or in court, another sophist often stands against the sophist, using the same technique in the opposite direction. Then it is decided which story is more consistent and psychologically acceptable.

In practice, therefore, sophism in the field of the unprovable functions as an architecture of persuasion: where there are no material foundations, it builds a construction from probability, language, and the psychology of the listener, which stands firmly enough for people to accept it as true.

Since it is impossible for anyone to return to the past and verify what happened millennia ago, the relationship between Sanskrit and Western languages is an extremely grateful example for the application of sophistic principles.

Western linguists built on existing beliefs, values, and fears that dictated to Western Europeans that other nations were less worthy than themselves, and this task was of course most easily accomplished by university professors with great authority.

PIE Theory

Thus, European linguists of the 19th century reframed Sanskrit from the grandfather of many languages into one of the cousins. In place of the grandfather, they drew a hitherto unknown language of a hitherto unknown people, which they called Proto-Indo-Europeans (PIE). They intensively studied Sanskrit rules and reinterpreted them in terms of temporal development from a supposed Proto-Indo-European language. Phonetic differences between Greek and Latin and Sanskrit were, wherever possible, reinterpreted so that Greek and Latin sounds were attributed higher age, and Sanskrit sounds were marked as later innovations.

To make it all convincing, they had to attribute a much younger origin to Sanskrit itself. The age milestone, for their explanation to be even theoretically feasible, became the year 1500–1300 BC for the creation of the Rigveda. In the Rigveda itself, there are astronomical data that clearly indicate an age of at least 6000 years, which with one stroke annihilates the entire construction of Western linguistics.

What is this Western construction of language development, which is often labeled as "official" or at least described as "accepted by the majority of experts" or "most likely according to the opinion of the vast majority of experts"?

In short, it says that around 4000 or 3500 BC, steppe peoples with horses came to Europe and brought with them the Proto-Indo-European (PIE) language, which everyone supposedly accepted because they feared these steppe horsemen or wanted to imitate them. The same supposedly happened in India, and that is why the languages are supposedly similar. In this sense, the Proto-Indo-Europeans in India supposedly had a similar role as the British attributed to themselves in India, seeing themselves as bringers of enlightenment and development, without considering that British colonization of India was catastrophic for India, as it fell from a world power to the level of the third world, while Britain, conversely, grew from a third-world country into a world power.

Where do the differences between, say, Germanic, Slavic, and Romance languages come from according to PIE theory? According to them, the Germans and Romans (and Celts and Greeks) with their languages were the first to leave the ancestral homeland in the steppes and dominate Europe. Then radical linguistic changes supposedly began to occur, but not so much in Europe, where different languages supposedly met; there the locals supposedly simply accepted the new language in its original form without further ado. In the steppe "ancestral homeland," meanwhile, dramatic phonetic changes supposedly occurred, just like that, without any contact with anyone, all by themselves. And precisely while these changes were supposedly happening, those who remained in the steppe "ancestral homeland" supposedly split further. Some supposedly went to India and Persia, where again all millions of local inhabitants supposedly accepted their steppe language completely without resistance and quickly developed it into the most perfect language in the world and wrote the most extensive and complex mythology in the world. Others supposedly went to the Baltic and developed the amber trade there, while a third group supposedly hid in the Pripyat marshes for long millennia and crawled out only in the Middle Ages, just when there was war throughout Europe between Rome and Persian allies (Goths, Sarmatians, Avars, etc.). At the moment when these marsh-dwellers supposedly began to spread across Europe, their language supposedly began to disintegrate into languages and dialects, until, for example, in Slovenia there were more than 40 dialects, as we have today.

Of course, given the fact that the past is not easily provable, this is a completely legitimate theory. The question is only whether it is convincing.

Assuming that the Rigveda is actually probably only 6000 years old, such an explanation immediately falls apart as nonsensical, since the steppe horsemen came to India only a little before 1300-1500 BC.

But how did the date 1500 BC for the Rigveda actually come about? Well, it should be said that this is the idea of Max Müller, who himself admitted that this is merely his opinion and actually a guess.

Professor of Sanskrit De Saussure is often taken as proof of the correctness of PIE theory; he believed that the language from which the Indo-European languages must have arisen should have had, before certain vowels, some guttural sounds similar to the sound H. He came to this conviction when looking for reasons why certain words in Sanskrit have the sound A or a semivowel, while Greek and Latin have the vowel O, E, or A in the same places (in related words). Since he was convinced that Sanskrit was younger, he therefore assumed that the ancestor of all these languages had three types of such guttural sounds, which disappeared in all descendant languages but influenced the pronunciation of the vowels that remained. Thus he concluded that Sanskrit lost that guttural sound and additionally unified all vowels into A, while Latin and Greek supposedly also lost that guttural sound but at the same time supposedly preserved the original vowel, and therefore supposedly still have words today that "preserved" the original A's, E's, or O's in Latin and Greek, but not in Sanskrit. How logical it is to conclude that Sanskrit at some point degraded, while Latin and Greek supposedly preserved the old forms, is of course a question in itself, especially since Sanskrit has obviously preserved a whole host of ancient archaisms that Greek and Latin did not, but let us leave that aside for now. What is important is the fact that a few years later they deciphered the Hittite language and actually found some guttural sounds before vowels there. But no one was interested that they found only one such guttural sound, while De Saussure predicted three similar sounds. One sound, namely, has no influence on the difference between O's, E's, and A's and is useless for De Saussure's thesis. But this is obviously not so important, since for the last hundred years the mantra has been persistently repeated that De Saussure predicted Hittite laryngeals, which supposedly "proved" the laryngeal theory. This says that Anatolian languages are the oldest Indo-European languages, followed in age by Western European languages, and the youngest according to this logic would of course be Sanskrit, Iranian, Baltic, and Slavic languages. Of these, the oldest would be Baltic, then Indo-Iranian, and of course completely at the end, Slavic languages.

How Does the Integral Theory View Language Development?

The Integral Theory is actually very simple. It is based on verifying the possibility that the dating of the Rigveda actually corresponds to the astronomical formulation in the Rigveda itself, which Western science simply does not take seriously, saying that the ancient Indians could not have been so precise. Which is not exactly an impregnable argument.

If we therefore hypothetically assume that the Rigveda is about 6000 years old, we see that this date coincides with an older migration to Europe, namely the one that brought the Starčevo culture to the Balkans.

The wave from the steppes would therefore be only the next wave. If we calculate that the Rigveda was created 6000 years ago, then it seems reasonable to assume that the Vedic culture also had other colonies, primarily for trade purposes, for example in the steppes north of the Black Sea, which extend practically to the Baltic. That the Harappan culture traded with the Mediterranean is, for example, already proven, as Indian weights have been found in Crete and in the Danube Delta. But according to PIE theory, there was supposedly no contact between the Harappan culture and the Vedic culture, which is again strange, since the Harappan culture supposedly lasted practically until the Vedic one, which has risen not far from the territory of the Harappan culture.

The steppe migration occurred twice, as we can see today from genetics; in the east, the R1a haplogroup predominates, and in the west, R1b. R1a is often attributed to "Slavdom." But in reality, this is much more likely originally a Baltic group. The R1b haplogroup is associated with Kartvelian (Caucasian) peoples.

Such a view completely satisfactorily explains the differences between Satem eastern and Centum western European languages.

Western Centum languages did not "preserve" the original pronunciation; rather, they simplified it due to mixing with Kartveloid speeches.

An additional migration wave that influenced European languages was the Anatolian wave around 1300-1500 BC to the northern Adriatic, which somewhat influenced Slavic languages and Latin, but above all its influence was philosophical-religious, as it brought to Europe the worship of fire and the Urnfield culture.

Slavic languages according to the integral model therefore originate from a Vedic dialect (prakrit) with certain influences of Balkan aborigines (yotation), with later influences of Baltic (steppe influence) and Anatolian (Luwian) languages. They came to the Balkans approximately with the Starčevo culture, where they acquired yotation. As they spread northward, over time they met the Baltic-speaking Corded Ware culture, from which related dialects developed, from which various Slavic languages later emerged.

Unifications of dialects also followed, first with the Anatolian migration in the middle of 1.st millennium BC, which was not numerous but was important because it originated from the prestigious Luwian culture. The next unification occured in the Avar state, and the last unification with Church Slavonic.
